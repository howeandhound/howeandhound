Ollie's Law

Howe & Hound is committed to following Massachusetts' Ollie’s Law (Chapter 213 of the Acts of 2024), which establishes safety and oversight requirements for dog daycare and boarding facilities.

In accordance with Massachusetts requirements, all dogs must have a current license issued by their town or city of residence, and proof of licensing must be provided before attending day camp or boarding.

We prioritize safe handling, appropriate supervision, and responsible group play. Every dog is evaluated individually, and we reserve the right to separate dogs, modify activities, or decline services when necessary to protect the health and safety of everyone in our care.
